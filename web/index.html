          How it works

          1. NLP entity extraction: synonyms, typo-tolerant fuzzy matching, negation scope ("no fever"), duration rules.
          2. Neural layer: a Random Forest + Decision Tree soft-voting ensemble scores 34 conditions.
          3. Symbolic layer: a disease–symptom knowledge graph and clinical rules validate, re-rank or reject each prediction. If nothing is supported, it abstains instead of guessing.
          4. RAG: TF-IDF retrieval over a curated knowledge base produces a cited explanation.
          5. Geospatial: hospitals and clinics from OpenStreetMap, ranked by distance, specialty match, emergency readiness and data quality.
